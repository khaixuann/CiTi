Questions

Good to know.

Where is CiTi made?

CiTi is made in Malaysia — you'll find “Made in Malaysia” on the label.

Is CiTi halal?

The jars carry the Halal logo on the label.

How should I store it?

Keep it in a cool, dry place. Once opened, refrigerate and use a clean, dry spoon each time.

Where can I buy it?

You can order CiTi online on Lazada or Shopee. Retailers and distributors are welcome to get in touch.

What sizes does it come in?

CiTi comes in two jar sizes: 240g and 450g.
